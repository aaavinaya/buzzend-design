# Google Flow prompts — seven 10-second exercise clips (2026-10-16)

Use: social (Reels, Shorts, TikTok), website hero, Play feature video background. Not for the App Store app preview — Apple only accepts captured app footage there.

## Flow settings (same for every clip)
- Mode: Frames to Video, upload the matching image from `Workout-image-with landmark` as the first frame.
- Aspect ratio 9:16, 1080×1920 or higher. Generate 8 s, then Extend once and trim to 10 s, or set 10 s if your plan allows.
- Audio: room tone only (prompt says so). Replace with the app's spoken count in the edit if you want the numbers heard.
- Generate 3–4 takes per exercise and keep the one where limbs stay solid and the skeleton stays on the joints.
- The big white count: generated digits often warp. Keep the number static in the prompt, or mask it and add the real count in the edit.

## Shared style block (paste before each motion block)
Same man as the reference frame: short dark hair, trimmed beard, heather-grey t-shirt, black shorts, black trainers. Bright home gym, light oak floor, soft grey wall, dumbbell rack on the right, soft daylight from a window on the left. Locked-off static phone camera, no zoom, no pan, no handheld shake. Thin orange skeleton lines with white joint dots stay locked to his joints through every rep; the exercise label stays at the top; the large white number stays exactly as shown. Realistic motion, natural breathing, no slow motion. Ends in the same position as the first frame so the clip loops. Quiet room tone, no music, no speech. No extra people, no camera movement, no text changes, no morphing limbs.

## Motion blocks

**1. Squats (front, `squats.jpg`)**
Camera at knee height facing him. He performs four slow bodyweight squats in 10 seconds: hips sit back and down until thighs are parallel to the floor, knees track over the feet, chest stays tall, hands clasped in front; he drives back up to a full stand each time. Smooth, controlled tempo, two seconds down, two seconds up.

**2. Squats (side, `squat-side-wise.jpg`)**
Camera side-on at knee height, two steps away. Four squats in 10 seconds with arms extended straight forward at shoulder height; hips travel back and down to parallel and return to standing, heels stay planted, back stays neutral.

**3. Push-ups (`pushup.jpg`)**
Camera on the floor, slightly in front and to the side, looking across at him. Five push-ups in 10 seconds: body straight from head to heels, chest lowers until just above the floor, arms press back to full lock-out, hips never sag or pike.

**4. Sit-ups (`situp.jpg`)**
Camera side-on at floor level. Five sit-ups in 10 seconds: arms crossed on the chest, knees bent, feet flat; he curls up until his torso is upright, then lowers under control until the shoulder blades touch the mat.

**5. Lunges (`lunge.jpg`)**
Camera side-on at knee height. Four alternating forward lunges in 10 seconds: a long step forward, back knee lowers to just above the mat, front shin vertical, chest up, hands on hips, then a push back to standing before the other leg steps.

**6. Jumping jacks (`jumping-jacks.jpg`)**
Camera facing him at hip height, whole body in frame. Eight jumping jacks in 10 seconds: feet jump wide while arms swing fully overhead until the hands nearly touch, then feet together and arms down to the sides, light on the toes, steady rhythm.

**7. High knees (`high-knee.jpg`)**
Camera facing him at hip height, whole body in frame. Fast high knees for 10 seconds: each knee lifts to hip height, opposite arm drives forward, tall posture, quick light footfalls, about three steps per second, staying on the same spot.

**8. Burpees (`burpee.jpg`)**
Use the standing jump figure as the first frame (crop out the ghosted figures) or use the image as an Ingredient instead of a first frame. Camera facing him at hip height, three steps away. Three burpees in 10 seconds: from standing he squats, places both hands on the floor, kicks the feet back into a straight plank, hops the feet back in, stands and finishes with a small jump, arms overhead; lands softly and repeats.
