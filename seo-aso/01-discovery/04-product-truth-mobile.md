## 2. How pose detection works, what leaves the phone, permission copy

### 2.1 Model and pipeline
- **On-device Google MediaPipe PoseLandmarker (BlazePose), 33 landmarks**, on both platforms. Android bundles `pose_landmarker_full.task` (~9.4 MB; `androidResources { noCompress += "task" }`, AND `app/build.gradle.kts:222–226`; size in `docs/release/PROD_RELEASE_PLAN.md` §1.6). iOS bundles `pose_landmarker_lite.task` (`Buzzend/Features/AIWorkout/PoseDetection/PoseKitBridge/MediaPipePoseSource.swift:53`; file in `Features/AIWorkout/Resources/`), via CocoaPods `MediaPipeTasksVision` (IOS `Podfile`). **Not ML Kit** (Flutter used ML Kit — AND `docs/reference/APP_FLOW.md` §7), **not Apple Vision** (the earlier Vision pipeline *"has been removed"* — IOS `docs/WorkoutEngine/README.md`).
- Counting engine: Android Kotlin `feature/pose/engine/*` (ingress gate → One-Euro smoothing → per-exercise analyzer → 5-state machine → `RepValidator` weighted score ROM/posture/stability/tempo/confidence). iOS `PoseKit/` is a *"byte-exact Swift port"* with 141 parity tests (IOS `docs/WorkoutEngine/README.md`). Known model asymmetry: iOS lite vs Android full (IOS `CHALLENGE_SYSTEM_DESIGN.md` §13).

### 2.2 What is uploaded — the pose trace (the privacy-critical fact)
- **No camera frames or video are uploaded by the counting pipeline.** But every counted set is written as a JSONL *pose trace* — per frame: all 33 normalized landmarks + world landmarks, gate verdict, phase, every candidate rep incl. rejected ones with `cheat_measures`; plus identity block `clientTraceId, appVersion, environment, deviceModel, osVersion` — and uploaded after the set to `POST /v1/rep-logs` (multipart; fields workoutType, workoutDate, repCount, appVersion, platform, file). Server key `ai-rep-logs/<userId>/<date>/<uuid>.json`, attributed to the authenticated user. Typical 2–5 MB per set; sent over Wi-Fi **or mobile data**. (AND `docs/pose/traces/POSE_TRACE_COLLECTION_STATE.md` §1, §3; `docs/pose/traces/POSE_TRACE_BUILD_GATE.md` §5; `docs/marketing/POSE_TRACE_DISCLOSURE_GAP.md` §1.)
- **Gating is compile-time, not a user switch:** Android `BuildConfig.POSE_TRACE` per flavor; iOS `#if POSE_TRACE` via `POSE_TRACE_CONDITION` (pbxproj:774). *"There is no in-app switch and no tester opt-in — collection is silent, and the updated terms rather than a checkbox are what cover it."* (POSE_TRACE_BUILD_GATE.md intro.)
- **Production status — collects on BOTH platforms:**
  - Android `main` defaults prod OFF (`app/build.gradle.kts:175` → `poseTraceOverride ?: false`) **but the release line defaults prod ON since 3.1.4 (2026-08-28)**: release gradle line 389 `buildConfigField("boolean", "POSE_TRACE", (poseTraceOverride ?: true).toString())` under the prod flavor, with the comment *"Pose session-trace collection: ON by default for prod as of 3.1.4 (the user's call …) Landmark coordinates only — never pixels, never video. Collection is covered by the terms rather than by an in-app switch"* (lines 376–389). A silent R8 bug meant *"no trace ever left a phone"* until fixed in 3.1.7 (BUILD_GATE §5).
  - iOS: `POSE_TRACE` on all four configurations since **2026-09-09**, Prod included (AND `app/build.gradle.kts:71–74`; IOS `docs/POSE_TRACE_LOGGING.md`: *"Every counted set is recorded … and uploaded silently … Nothing about it is visible to a user: no switch, no list, no prompt, no share."*).
- **Disclosure status:** `docs/marketing/POSE_TRACE_DISCLOSURE_GAP.md` — the only policy text in any repo (Flutter's embedded copy) covers *"Pictures and videos … the content that you post"*; searching it for pose/landmark/body position/skeletal/joint/motion/biometric finds nothing; Play Data safety and App Store privacy label almost certainly do not declare it; biometric classification is an open legal question. The 3.1.14 release gradle (lines 231–234) restates: *"prod ships with POSE_TRACE on … and as of this release also reports identified analytics events. Neither the terms nor the privacy policy mentions either."*
- The iOS `CoachProfileStore` and the trace are different things: the former is local-only.

### 2.5 What would make "your video never leaves your phone" false
1. **Body-landmark traces are uploaded for every production set, on both platforms, with no opt-out** (above). A sentence like "nothing from your camera leaves your phone" would be false — exactly why the Day-2 listing draft removed its privacy paragraph (`docs/marketing/day-2/STORE_LISTING_DRAFT.md` "Why there is no privacy paragraph").
2. Posting uploads the video+audio to S3/CloudFront (`https://d21ho8qsn272oh.cloudfront.net/` prod) and it is shown publicly in the feed/challenge.
3. The analytics layer sends identified events (Firebase uid) since 3.1.7 (§8).
4. Narrower claim that *is* literally true against the code: camera **images/video** are never uploaded unless you post. Even so, the marketing docs recommend no privacy claim until the terms/Data-safety owner signs it off (`ACTION_ITEMS.md` A6).

- **Daily goal:** daily **step goal** (`PATCH /v1/settings/step-goal`, chip grid in Settings; Home "Daily goal" tile). Height/weight are stored **device-locally** only (Settings 13.2 in FEATURE_TEST_MATRIX) and feed calories.

- **Calories:** computed client-side in AND `core/util/WorkoutMetrics.kt` — steps via a walking-time × MET model with stride factor 0.414 (Flutter parity, "deliberately inconsistent" constants preserved); reps via per-rep seconds × MET (e.g. BURPEES MET 8.0 at 3.0 s/rep, HIGH_KNEES MET 8.0 at 0.75 s/rep), using height/weight. Shown on the set result ("duration + kcal"), profile Stats drill-down, share card, and Full Body results. Full Body uses a *different* per-rep kcal table (squat 0.45, pushup 0.35, burpee 0.75, jumping 0.25, highknee 0.22 — DESIGN `full-body-workout-implementation-plan.md` §2.1). Treat calorie figures as estimates in copy.

## 12. iOS `deep-research-report.md` and `CHALLENGE_SYSTEM_DESIGN.md`

- **`deep-research-report.md` (530 lines)** — a research spec for adding **sit-up, jumping jack and lunge** tracking **using Apple's Vision framework**, modelled on an app that "already includes a squat module": camera at chest-to-waist height 1.8–2.0 m, frontal/diagonal view; biomechanical primary signals (trunk flexion for sit-ups; arm/leg abduction for jacks; front-knee flexion + hip drop for lunges) with clinical angle ranges; per-exercise state machines. **Status: SUPERSEDED / historical.** The shipped iOS app uses MediaPipe + the PoseKit port of Android's engine, and the Vision pipeline (`VisionPoseProvider`, `SquatEngine`, `PushupEngine`, `RepKit`) *"has been removed"* (IOS `docs/WorkoutEngine/README.md`). Product-relevant takeaways that still hold: full-body framing is required; front view for jumping jacks, side/diagonal for lunges; the app should prompt to reposition — all of which the shipped coach does.
- **`CHALLENGE_SYSTEM_DESIGN.md` (253 lines)** — *"Design document. No implementation."* Confirmed decisions: real-world prizes need the strongest anti-cheat; "integrity + review gate" verification; lean V1 = **Verified Leaderboard challenge + async 1v1 Battle**. Defines the **Verified Session** record (engine reps, live-camera-only, camera facing, trace id, per-rep validator rollups, burned-in video, server integrity score/flags, PROVISIONAL/VERIFIED/FLAGGED/REJECTED), **Casual / Competitive / Prize tiers**, server-side heuristics + duplicate-trace detection, human review before payout, explicit `scoringRule` (CUMULATIVE_REPS / BEST_SET / later TIME_TO_TARGET), provisional-then-final boards, and the backend work in `java-itg-gochall`. Open risks: legal (skill-contest framing, region/age), privacy of prod pose traces, iOS-lite vs Android-full model variance, review SLA. **Everything in it is PLANNED/DESIGN-ONLY.** Its §14 "current-state reference" is the most concise, accurate description of what *is* implemented (reps → `POST /v1/workouts` count-only; cumulative-sum leaderboards; Beat-It ranking rule; post-tag-only attribution; thresholds; five exercises + steps) — though its line "pose trace compiled OUT in prod" is **stale**: both platforms collect in prod since 3.1.4 / 2026-09-09.
- Also in IOS `docs/`: `BUZZEND_MARKETING_STRATEGY.md` (Aug 2026; states features "confirmed in the codebase", positions Buzzend as "the free AI workout that counts your reps with just your phone, and lets you challenge your friends"; its feature table says sit-up and lunge demos were "not shipped yet" — now stale, both shipped 2026-08-19 on Android and always on iOS); `POSE_TRACE_LOGGING.md` (the silent iOS trace upload, §2.2 above); `CHAT_E2EE_ANALYSIS.md`/`CHAT_E2EE_BACKEND_SPEC.md` (why 1:1 history vanished and the key-rule redesign); `qa/IOS_SWEEP_2026-10-05_TRIAGE.md` (iOS has no multi-select send-to sheet; Discover lacks an offline error state).
