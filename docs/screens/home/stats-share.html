Profile Stats — the header fix + sharing a period

Two changes to the Stats section on Profile. One, the “Today” pill becomes an icon — it has to, because in Week and Month mode it doesn’t jump to today, it jumps to this week / this month, so the word is wrong two thirds of the time. Two, Stats gets a share action. A single day is exactly the I · Sticker board card we just picked for Home, fed from Stats data instead of the Home tile. Week and Month need their own cards — a week isn’t “one workout”, so the numbers that carry it are different. Eight candidates each below: four in the sticker-board language so they match the day card, and four in the other languages from the Share-your-day deck, because a period summary may want a different voice from a one-day brag.

1The header row

Faithful to ProfileStatsSection.ChartHeader — the two nav arrows, the centred period title, the jump-to-current affordance, and the calendar picker. The share button is new. Tap an icon candidate to swap it into the “after” header.

2Day — reuse I · Sticker board

Identical to the Home card, only the source changes: DayStat.counts + caloriesByDate instead of the Home tile’s DailyGoal. Steps lead when the day has them; a reps-only day promotes calories to the hero and the seal shows the type count instead of goal %. One card, no variations needed.

3Week — sticker board family

A week has seven days of shape that a day card has nowhere to put. Each candidate spends that shape differently: as chips, as a chart, as a single best-day story, or as ranked totals.

4Week — the other design languages

The same week, same numbers, in four of the languages from the Share-your-day deck. Worth a look even if you keep sticker board for the day card — a period summary is a different kind of object from a day brag, and it may well want a different voice. WA and WB in particular carry more numbers per square inch than any sticker layout can, because they aren’t spending space on borders and rotation.

5Month — sticker board family

A month is too long for per-day bars to stay readable at 1080px wide, so three of the four lead with consistency rather than volume — which is also the more flattering story for most months.

6Month — the other design languages

A month is where the non-sticker languages pull ahead hardest. MB · Receipt is the surprise: a month genuinely is an itemised statement, so the till-receipt format stops being a gimmick and starts being the honest shape of the data. MA · Poster is the one people screenshot.

8Decisions taken

No streak on any share card. Streak is 100% backend-computed (GET /v1/streaks/status) with freezes, missed-day restore and invite-to-restore — StreakModels.kt says outright that the math is never reimplemented client-side. Stats doesn’t load it, so a card could only show a locally recomputed “longest run of non-empty days”, which would be wrong three ways: it ignores freezes and restores, it truncates at the period boundary, and — the one that actually bites — ProfileScreen already renders the real currentStreak as “Day streak” a few hundred pixels above the Stats section, so the two numbers would contradict each other on the same screen. Every card above now uses days active as its consistency figure and strongest on ⟨weekday⟩ where a second consistency line was wanted; both are period-scoped, unambiguous, and derived from data Stats already holds.

9The open question, answered

Ship daily only, first?

No — but ship it first. Day is free (the Home card already exists, it only needs a different data source), so it can land in the same change as the header icon. Week and Month are a second, separate change once you pick a candidate below.

Why not one card for all three?

The hero number has no honest equivalent. A day’s hero is “8,240 steps”. A month’s hero as a raw total (“186,400 steps”) is a number nobody has intuition for, and it makes a quiet month look like a failure. Consistency (“24 of 31 days”) reads well at any volume.

What about an empty period?

Suppress the share button entirely when hasNoData — the Stats section already computes it. There is no dignified share card for a zero week, and offering one invites the user to post it.
